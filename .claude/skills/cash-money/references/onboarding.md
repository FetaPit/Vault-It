# Setup interview

Run this the first time someone uses Cash Money. Aim for one or two short messages from you, not a form. Finish with one quick win in the same chat.

## Step 1. Profile

Ask only what you don't already know:

1. Business name, what it sells, and who to (businesses, the public, or both)
2. Structure: sole trader, Ltd, partnership, not started
3. Trading start date. Ltd: incorporation date and year end
4. This year: expected sales and profit (rough)
5. Other income: job, rental, other dividends
6. VAT: registered? scheme? rolling 12-month sales
7. Staff and payroll: none, director only, or employees
8. Scottish taxpayer?
9. Who does the books now: owner, bookkeeper, accountant? Who files year-end?

## Step 2. Systems check

Find out what's connected and what isn't. Use `ListConnectors` if available.

| Tool | Why it matters | If missing |
|---|---|---|
| Bank (Tide) | Cash and transactions | CSV export each month |
| Xero | Live P&L, balance sheet, aged debtors | Strongly recommend for a Ltd |
| Stripe / card reader | Sales and fees | Payout reports |
| Gmail or Outlook | Chase emails, learning their tone, receipts | Draft emails in chat to copy |
| Google Calendar | Deadline reminders | List deadlines in the brief |
| Google Drive / Sheets | Receipts, invoice lists, trackers | Upload files to the chat |

## Step 3. The money bottleneck

Ask: "What's the one money job you hate most, or worry about most?" Map the answer to the outcome map in `SKILL.md`. Common ones:

- Chasing late payers → credit control
- Not knowing if cash will run out → 13-week forecast
- Tax surprises → tax pot and deadlines
- Messy books → bookkeeping pass
- Paying yourself → salary and dividends plan

## Step 4. Quick win (same chat)

Deliver one useful thing straight away:
- a chase list with drafts ready, or
- a tax pot figure with the next three deadlines, or
- a 13-week forecast with the lowest point, or
- a salary and dividend plan with the paperwork drafted.

## Step 5. Save and schedule

1. Save `cash-money-profile.md`: profile, connected tools, bottleneck, tone-of-voice notes, KPIs to track (cash, runway, money owed to you, overdue %, tax pot, rolling VAT turnover).
2. Offer the Monday money brief on a schedule (see `routines.md`).
3. Offer to add the next 3 deadlines to the calendar.

## Sector watch-outs

| Sector | Watch for |
|---|---|
| Construction | CIS deductions and monthly CIS returns; domestic reverse charge VAT |
| Hospitality | Tips and tronc, stock and wastage, cash takings records |
| Agencies and contractors | IR35 on contracts; retainers and work in progress |
| E-commerce | Marketplace VAT, stock valuation, platform fees, returns |
| Property | Rental income rules, Section 24 for individuals, rent arrears chasing |
| Consultancy | Late payment from large clients; expenses rebilled to clients |
