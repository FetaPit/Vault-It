# UK deadlines for small businesses

Rule: flag anything due within 60 days of today. If a date falls on a weekend, HMRC generally still expects it by that date for online filings.

## Every year: sole traders

| Deadline | What |
|---|---|
| 5 October | Register for Self Assessment, if this is your first tax year |
| 31 October | Paper tax return |
| 31 January | Online tax return, balancing payment, 1st payment on account |
| 31 July | 2nd payment on account |
| MTD quarters (standard) | 7 Aug, 7 Nov, 7 Feb, 7 May for quarters ending 5 Jul, 5 Oct, 5 Jan, 5 Apr |
| MTD final declaration | 31 January after the tax year |

Late filing: £100 straight away, more after 3 months. MTD for Income Tax uses penalty points for late updates.

## Every year: limited companies

| Deadline | What |
|---|---|
| Within 3 months of starting to trade | Register for Corporation Tax |
| 9 months + 1 day after year end | Pay Corporation Tax |
| 9 months after year end | File accounts at Companies House (first accounts: 21 months after incorporation) |
| 12 months after year end | File CT600 with HMRC |
| Each year, by review date + 14 days | Confirmation statement |
| 9 months after year end | Director's loan must be repaid to avoid s455 |

Late accounts at Companies House: £150 to £1,500 for private companies, doubled if late two years running.

## VAT

| Deadline | What |
|---|---|
| Within 30 days of the month you pass £90k (rolling 12 months) | Register |
| 1 month + 7 days after each VAT period | File return and pay |

## Payroll

| Deadline | What |
|---|---|
| On or before each payday | Full Payment Submission (RTI) |
| 22nd of next month (19th by cheque) | Pay PAYE and NI to HMRC |
| 31 May | P60s to employees |
| 6 July | P11D and P11D(b) |
| 22 July | Class 1A NI on benefits |
| From the first employee's start date | Pension auto-enrolment duties begin. Assess and enrol eligible staff within 6 weeks |
