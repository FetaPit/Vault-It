# UK rates and thresholds, 2026/27

Tax year 6 April 2026 to 5 April 2027. Checked November 2026. For any other year, check gov.uk first.
Figures marked (check) weren't confirmed against a primary source when this was written.

## Income tax (England, Wales, Northern Ireland)

| Band | Taxable income | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Personal Allowance | £12,570 | 0% |
| Basic | next £37,700 (to £50,270 total) | 20% |
| Higher | to £125,140 | 40% |
| Additional | over £125,140 | 45% |

- Personal Allowance drops £1 for every £2 of income over £100,000. Gone at £125,140.
- Allowances and bands are frozen until April 2031.
- Scotland has its own bands for salary, trading and rental income. Dividend and savings rates follow the UK table.

## National Insurance

| Type | Rate |
|---|---|
| Class 4 (sole traders) | 6% on profits £12,570–£50,270, 2% above |
| Class 2 | No longer compulsory. Voluntary contributions available below the small profits threshold to protect State Pension (check weekly rate) |
| Class 1 employee | 8% on £12,570–£50,270, 2% above |
| Class 1 employer | 15% above £5,000 a year (secondary threshold) |
| Employment Allowance | Up to £10,500 off employer NI. Not available if the only employee is a director paid above the secondary threshold |

## Corporation Tax

| Profits | Rate |
|---|---|
| Up to £50,000 | 19% (small profits rate) |
| £50,000–£250,000 | 25% less marginal relief (effective ~26.5% on this slice) |
| Over £250,000 | 25% |

- Marginal relief = 3/200 × (£250,000 − profit).
- Limits are divided by (1 + number of associated companies) and cut pro rata for periods shorter than 12 months.
- Pay 9 months and 1 day after year end. File CT600 within 12 months.

## Director's loans

- s455 tax: 35.75% on loans made on or after 6 April 2026, 33.75% on loans made 6 April 2022 to 5 April 2026. Due if still overdrawn 9 months after year end. Refunded after repayment.
- Loans over £10,000 at any point in the year are a taxable benefit unless interest is charged at HMRC's official rate.
