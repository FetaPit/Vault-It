# Bookkeeping categories

These match the boxes on the Self Assessment self-employment pages (SA103F), so a sole trader's year-end totals drop straight in. A limited company uses the same groups plus the extra rows at the bottom.

## Income

| Category | What goes here |
|---|---|
| Sales | Money from customers, Stripe and card reader payouts (gross up Stripe: sales = payout + fees) |
| Other business income | Grants, refunds from suppliers, interest on business savings (Ltd) |
| Not income | Owner putting money in (capital or director's loan), loans, transfers between own accounts, HMRC refunds |

## Expenses (allowable)

| Category | SA103F box | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Cost of goods | Cost of goods bought for resale | Stock, materials, packaging, print-on-demand costs |
| Subcontractors | Construction industry payments | CIS subcontractors only |
| Wages and staff | Wages, salaries and other staff costs | Staff pay, employer NI, employer pension, freelancers |
| Travel | Car, van and travel expenses | Trains, flights, hotels, parking, Uber, mileage at 45p |
| Premises | Rent, rates, power and insurance | Office or studio rent, business rates, business insurance, use of home |
| Repairs | Repairs and maintenance | Fixing equipment or premises (not improvements) |
| Phone, software and office | Phone, fax, stationery and other office costs | Phone, broadband share, software subscriptions, domains, hosting, stationery, postage |
| Advertising | Advertising and business entertainment (entertaining not allowable) | Google and Meta ads, printing, sponsorships, website design |
| Interest | Interest on bank and other loans | Business loan or overdraft interest |
| Bank and payment fees | Bank, credit card and other financial charges | Tide fees, Stripe and PayPal fees, card reader fees |
| Bad debts | Irrecoverable debts written off | Invoices you've given up on (only if already counted as income) |
| Professional fees | Accountancy, legal and other professional fees | Accountant, solicitor, Companies House fees |
| Other | Other business expenses | Trade subscriptions, training, ICO data fee, small tools |
| Equipment | Capital allowances (not an expense line) | Laptops, cameras, machinery, vans. Claim through AIA |

## Not allowable

| Item | Where it goes |
|---|---|
| Personal spending | Drawings (sole trader) or director's loan account (Ltd) |
| Client entertaining | Disallowed. Record it but add it back |
| Fines and penalties | Disallowed |
| Depreciation | Disallowed. Use capital allowances instead |
| Tax payments to HMRC | Not an expense. Sole trader: drawings. Ltd: pays the tax liability (PAYE and VAT clear their own balances) |

## Limited company extras

| Category | Notes |
|---|---|
| Directors' salaries | Expense. Run through payroll |
| Director's loan account | Money the director takes or puts in that isn't salary, dividend or expense repayment |
| Dividends | Not an expense. Paid out of post-tax profits |
| Corporation Tax | Not an expense |
| VAT control | If VAT registered, VAT on sales and purchases sits here, not in income or expenses |
