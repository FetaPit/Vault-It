How XP works
  • The roadmap is one path of 30 stages in order. Each stage has up to three steps, and every step earns XP: Starter 20, Solid 40, Strong 80, Summit 150.
  • Steps count once you reach their stage, so the path stays in order. A skipped stage earns nothing and costs nothing.
  • Badges and saving habits are side quests. They count in any order.
  • XP you earn stays earned, even if a figure drops later.
  • Twenty levels in five ranks: Groundwork, Shield, Growth, Keys and Freedom.
  • Reach Level 8, Vault Guard, and use Vault-It on 30 different days to claim one free month. We add it to your account by hand.
