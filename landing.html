Your data, plainly

No bank login, ever

You type everything in. Vault-It does not connect to your bank and never asks for your banking details.

Stored in the EU

Your account is held by Supabase in Frankfurt, encrypted in transit and at rest. It is not end-to-end encrypted. The privacy notice explains exactly who can see what.

Yours to take or delete

Download all your data, or delete your account and everything in it, yourself in Settings at any time.

Questions

What does it cost?

Nothing during the beta, and you never enter a card to join. Paid plans after the beta will start at £5.99 a month, and beta testers will hear about one-payment licence pricing first.

Do I have to connect my bank?

No. You type in your own figures, so there is no bank connection to set up or worry about.

What do I need to use it?

A phone, tablet or computer with a modern browser. It runs in the browser, and you can add it to your home screen like an app.

Is this financial advice?

No. Vault-It helps you organise your own numbers and see where they lead. It does not recommend products, and for big decisions you should speak to a regulated adviser.

What happens to my email address?

We send one email to confirm it’s you. After that you get tester emails only if you ticked that box, and news only if you ticked that one. Every email has a link to leave the list. If you never confirm, your request is deleted after 30 days.

When can I start?

Once you confirm your address, you get the link to the beta straight away.
