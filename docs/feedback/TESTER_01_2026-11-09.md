# Tester 1 feedback, 9 Nov 2026 (three days of use)

Source: the owner's Google Sheet "Vault-It" (tabs 1st Day, 2nd Day, 3rd Day), shared 9 Nov 2026 evening ("feedback from a tester"). Tester's own words are quoted; the action column is the Exec Assistant's proposal for the owner to accept, change or drop. Nothing here is built yet.

The tester is thoughtful and specific. Their big message, in their words: "after a few hours of browsing the website, any user should be able to understand the different functions ... for relatively a new user, it takes more time (than needed) to connect the dots."

## Everything else, by screen

### Onboarding and tour
1. Shorten the Pioneer line. From "You're among the first to vault. That means you're getting access before anyone else, and shaping what this becomes." To "You're among the first to vault, and shaping what this becomes."
2. Shorten the tour offer. Tester's final wording: "Want a 60-second tour? You can skip it now and access it later under ? Help".
3. Challenge icons: only Paying off debt earns its place; "Just the text will suffice?" Owner's call.
4. Tour step 3 copy ("What you own stands on the gold line and what you owe hangs below it. Tap a column to read it, drag to turn it.") should also sit on the 3D screen itself.
5. Step-level help should be reachable from each screen, not only inside the tour.
6. Numbered steps imply an order the app does not need. Step 12 (Quick View and Settings) is out of order. Suggestion: number only real procedures, describe the rest.

### Overview
7. Add the formula in plain sight: Net worth = (Assets + Surplus) - (Debt + Liabilities).
8. Positive figures green, negative red; group them.
9. Rename Total Assets, Total Debt, Total Liabilities to Assets, Debt, Liabilities. Drop "all holdings" if it duplicates Assets.
10. Give each sub-section a slightly different background so the eye can tell them apart.
11. Roadmap: how "Achieved" and its labels relate to "First log" is not self-explanatory. Wants clearer labels plus a PDF or video guide.
12. 3D: "consumes a lot of space, not that intuitive, comes in the way." Suggests moving it to the bottom like Precious Metals. Owner decision (3D net worth is on by default by the owner's word).
13. Dark background: "Have you considered a white/lighter background?" Daylight already exists; on day 3 the tester wrote "the color scheme looks vastly improved today". Likely they found the switch. Action: offer Night or Daylight during first run.
14. Help pop-up needs more contrast against the page behind it.
15. The "What does Net worth in 3D tell you that a normal graph does not?" pop-up intrudes mid-use. Move it out of the way or ask separately.

### Budget
16. Put "Choose a category" at the top, not the bottom.
17. Category picker: a dropdown with categories and sub-categories instead of one long list ("in a later release at least").
18. Remove the Committed essential (and sibling) headings at the top of the page: no link, no purpose, repeated below.
19. Try Income above Spending & saving instead of side by side; the page is cluttered.
20. Flow diagram: text far too small; the font shrinks as items grow. Keep a constant font and let the map grow taller; left-align so the right-hand labels fit.
21. Labels repeat four or five times; "% of income" and "EVERY £100 OF INCOME" say the same thing. Shrink or merge.
22. Spending mix vs income deserves its own small section: "very good guidance in general."
23. Overall: "very helpful and good, but cluttered. If redundant fields are removed or minimised, it's an excellent reference point."

### Other tabs
24. Crypto: "Does Refresh Live Prices work?" Confirm and say where prices come from.
25. Liabilities: Name and Value alone saved an entry with Rate, Dep and Run empty. Confirm that is by design, or make them required.
