# What is in Vault-It right now

I designed Vault-It for me first, because the tool I needed did not exist. Now it is in a free beta, and this is a tour of what you can use today. Each week after this, the "what changed" posts will cover only what is new.

## Set up in a few minutes

1. **Add your numbers.** Income, spending, debts, savings and what you own. You type them in, so nothing connects to your bank.
2. **See the whole picture.** Net worth over time, where your income goes, a debt payoff plan and the next step on your roadmap.
3. **Build the house.** Pick a house and a deposit target. It rises as you save, and fills in as you pay the mortgage down.

## What is in the beta

- **Overview.** Your net worth, assets and debts at a glance, with history as you update it.
- **Budget and cash flow.** Income and spending by category, with a diagram of where the money goes each month.
- **House Builder.** A 3D house that grows with your deposit: terraced, semi, detached, flat and more. Level 1 is saving the deposit. Level 2 is paying the mortgage down.
- **Assets and liabilities.** Cash, shares and funds, metals, crypto and possessions, next to what you owe.
- **Debt tracker.** Every debt in one list, a payoff order you choose (snowball or avalanche), and a projected debt-free date.
- **Roadmap.** Stages from clearing debt to owning your home, with milestones as you reach them.

## Your data, plainly

- **No bank login, ever.** You type everything in.
- **Stored in the EU.** Your account is held by Supabase in Frankfurt, encrypted in transit and at rest. It is not end-to-end encrypted.
- **Yours to take or delete.** Download all your data, or delete your account and everything in it, yourself in Settings at any time.

## What I ask of testers

- Use it on your phone or laptop. Made-up figures are fine.
- Tell me what is confusing, broken, slow or ugly.
- Answer the odd short question by email when I ask.

## One ask

Open it, add one debt and one saving, and reply with the first thing that confused you.
