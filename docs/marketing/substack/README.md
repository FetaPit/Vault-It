**Colours**

- Substack pages are light, so use the Daylight accent for links and buttons: **#006B67** (white text on it is easy to read).
- If a dark background is chosen: background **#080808**, accent **#44C0B9**, text **#E8E8E8**.
- Gold **#E8C56A** for highlights only, never for body text.

**Type.** Substack offers its own font list and does not take uploaded fonts. Pick a heavy geometric sans for headings (closest to Syne) and a clean sans for body text (closest to DM Sans). The header images carry the real brand fonts.

**Words** (brand book and the owner's rules)

- Name: **Vault-It**, with a hyphen. Tagline: **Get out of the red and into your home.** at the foot of every post.
- British English, sentence case, no em dashes, no exclamation marks, no hype words.
- Every post ends: one ask, the beta link, "Thanks, Pete", the author box, the tagline, then "Not regulated by the FCA. Not financial advice."

**Author box** (same on every post)

> Pete Thickett is a designer with neurodivergent traits and the founder of Vault-It. He runs PT Live Design in Lincoln, holds a Certified Professional in Psychology of Design from the London School of International Business, and is a local leader for the Interaction Design Foundation. He designed Vault-It for himself because it did not exist.
