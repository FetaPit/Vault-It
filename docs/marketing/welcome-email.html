The story

I was drowning in spreadsheets trying to get from debt to my first home. Every tool I found tracked one thing. None of them showed me the whole path. So I built the one I wished existed.

What it is for

Vault-It puts your debts, savings and house deposit on one map. You type in your own figures, and we never ask for your bank login. Every pound you save lays a brick on your house. What you owe sits below the ground. The aim: nobody has to guess whether they will ever own a home.
