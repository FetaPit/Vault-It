If money admin and your brain do not get on

Many people find it hard to keep up with money admin, and some of that is how attention and time work. Here is what the research says, and what it does not.

Money and ADHD. Studies link ADHD with more money difficulty. A Swedish registry study found more overdrafts and unpaid bills in adults with ADHD [1]. A smaller study of 45 adults with ADHD and 51 without found less saving and lower scores on financial tests [2]. These are links, not proof of cause.

Time. Reviews of the evidence find that people with ADHD tend to judge time less accurately [3]. If "later" feels vague, a saving goal is hard to hold on to. A date and a house you can see make later concrete.

Seeing the future. In lab tasks, people shown a realistic picture of their older self put more aside [4]. That work was not about ADHD, and later replications were mixed.

Vault-It is not a treatment and not a diagnosis tool. I cannot promise it will help you. I built it because seeing the whole path helped me, and I would like to find out with you whether it helps others too.
