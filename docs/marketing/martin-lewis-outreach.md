## What MSE says about pitches
- Companies cannot pay for space. Products are chosen "based on how good we think the product or service is", then the commercial team looks for an affiliate link afterwards.
- MSE "usually try to avoid going directly to, or building a relationship with, financial product providers themselves". Expect no reply unless the tool is clearly useful to their readers.
- Martin's own test for any tool is "UO", user outcomes: does the user get the right answer. Lead with what the user ends up with, not the features.
- Neither MSE nor Martin gives advice on personal circumstances; Knight Ayton and Goldbug do not pass on messages about MSE.

## How to pitch (one page, Pete's voice)
Lead with the problem and the person, not the product: a designer with neurodivergent traits who could not find a money app that worked for how his brain works, so built one. Then one line on what it does, the live demo link (no sign-up), the FCA line, and one clear ask. No figures we cannot stand behind, no testimonials, no scarcity.
