Some people find money admin harder than others, and some of that is how attention and time work. Here is what the research says, and what it does not.

Money and ADHD. Studies link ADHD with more money difficulty. A Swedish registry study found more overdrafts and unpaid bills in adults with ADHD [1]. A smaller study of 45 adults with ADHD and 51 without found less saving and lower scores on financial tests [2]. These are links, not proof of cause.

Time. In children and teenagers with ADHD, a review of 27 studies found time is judged less accurately [3]. I have not found the same review for adults, so I will not claim it. If "later" feels vague, a saving goal is hard to hold on to. Vault-It tries to make later concrete with a date and a house you can see.

Seeing the future. In lab tasks, people shown a realistic picture of their older self put more aside [4]. That work was not about ADHD, and later replications were mixed.
