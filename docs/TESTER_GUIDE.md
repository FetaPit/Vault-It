# Vault-It — beta tester guide

Thanks for trying Vault-It, a UK personal-finance app that turns your numbers into a clear road to a deposit, a debt-free date and a house you can watch being built.

**Open it:** https://app.vault-it.co (works on phone and desktop — add it to your home screen for the full-screen app)

## Getting in (about a minute)

1. Enter your email address and tap **Continue**.
2. We email you a **6-digit code** (check spam if it takes more than a minute). Type it in.
3. Choose a **6-digit PIN** (not something easy to guess like 123456 or 000000). From then on you sign in on any device with just your **email + PIN** — no code needed.
   - Five wrong PINs lock sign-in for 15 minutes. An email code always works as a fallback ("Forgot PIN?").

## What to try

- **Set-up questions** — answer honestly, but use figures you are comfortable with. Test data is fine.
- **Budget** — add income and spending, see the cash-flow diagram.
- **Debts and assets** — add a few, then tick *I've mapped every debt* to unlock the next step of the **Roadmap**.
- **House Builder** — pick a house, set a deposit target and add savings. The house is built as a ghost while you save the deposit; once you're in mortgage mode it fills with colour. Try the 3D view.
- **A second device** — sign in again with email + PIN and check your data is there.

## Tell us what you find

Anything confusing, broken, slow or ugly is useful, especially on your phone.

The quickest way: tap **Feedback** (bottom right, on every screen), write one sentence and press Send. The app adds the screen you are on, your device and your last few taps, and a picture of the screen with every figure hidden (untick it if you prefer). It never sends your figures. Now and then the app asks a one-tap "How was that?" after a key moment; answer only if you like.

During the beta the app also notes which screens and features you use and any errors (never your figures or what you type), so we can see where people get stuck. You can switch this off in Settings, Share usage.

Or email: **hello@vault-it.co**

## Your data

- It is saved to your account so it follows you between devices, and a copy is kept on the device you use.
- It is private to your account, encrypted in transit and at rest — but **not end-to-end encrypted**. Full details: **Settings → Your data → Privacy notice**.
- **Download a copy any time:** Settings → Your data → *Download my data*.
- **Delete everything:** Settings → Danger Zone → *Delete my account permanently* (asks for your PIN). It erases your account, your data and the copy on that device.
- This is a **beta** — things will change and can break. Nothing here is financial advice.
