THE INTERVIEWS · 10 UK FIRST-TIME BUYERS · 30 MINUTES EACH

Listen first. Show Vault-It last.

WHO · FROM WHERE

Renting, saving for a first home, and paying off at least one of: credit card, loan, overdraft or buy-now-pay-later. Found through the Vault-It beta waitlist (before they log in), the HelpBnk community and my Lincoln network.

THE QUESTIONS · NO PITCH UNTIL NUMBER 6 IS DONE

  1. Talk me through where you are with buying your first home.
  2. What are you paying off while you save? Which one worries you most?
  3. How do you keep track now: an app, bank pots, a spreadsheet, nothing? What do you hate about it?
  4. When did you last try to work out when you could buy? What happened?
  5. Have you ever linked your bank to a money app? Why, or why not?
  6. If you could fix one thing about getting your first home, what would it be?

Then show the demo at vault-it.co/demo-a and write down the first thing they say.
