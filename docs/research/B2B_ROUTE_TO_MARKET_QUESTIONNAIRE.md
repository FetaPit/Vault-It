# B2B route to market: owner questionnaire

Owner ask, 12 Nov 2026: a comprehensive questionnaire to understand the route to market, articulate the B2B licensing opportunity, and test the idea of Vault-It as a way to validate a buyer's affordability more fully than a credit check, and how that earns revenue.

This file holds questions only. It contains no new figures. Where a number appears it is quoted from a document already in the repo and says which one.

## How to answer

- Easiest on a phone: the answer page, https://claude.ai/artifact/7VM4he3jKrTexFiQeaSGNi (private to the owner; one question at a time, answers save as you type).
- Answer under each question in this file, or in chat by code ("B4: building societies first, because ..."). Voice notes are fine. Short answers are fine.
- Tag each answer **K** (I know this, I have evidence), **B** (I believe this, no evidence yet) or **G** (a guess). The B and G answers become the research and interview list.
- "Don't know" is a useful answer. Do not fill a gap with a figure you cannot source.
- First pass: answer only the questions marked **[core]** (30 of 206). They are enough for a first route-to-market draft. Come back for the rest.
- Where a question touches law or regulation it is a prompt for a specialist, not a statement of what the law says.

## What the repo already says (so you can confirm or correct it)

From `docs/plans/INVESTOR_ROADMAP.md`, `docs/briefs/PITCH_DECK_V4_NOTES.md` and `docs/research/SEIS_FCA_BROKER_RESEARCH.md`:

- Plan figures marked as yours: 20 Partner licences in Year 3; B2B at £52,500 of £66,900 in Year 1 and £625k of £721k in Year 3.
- Today Vault-It is a planning and calculation tool outside FCA regulation. It does not advise, arrange, broker or read bank data.
- The broker readiness pack is a hypothesis: user-entered, "not verified".
- "25% more into affordability" has no evidence behind it and stays out of everything until it does.
- Open decision: a tool for buyers, a tool for brokers and lenders, or becoming a broker. The broker route and SEIS pull against each other.

## E. The affordability validation idea

### E1. What it is

- **E1 [core]** Describe the product in two sentences as a lender would hear it. What do they get that they do not have today?
- **E2 [core]** What exactly does "more comprehensive than a credit check" mean to you? List what Vault-It would show that a credit file does not.
- **E3** Which of these is it: a tidy pack of what the buyer says, verified evidence of what the buyer does, a score, a readiness stage, a forecast, or a recommendation?
- **E4** Which of these would it include: income, committed spending, flexible spending, savings rate, deposit progress and source, debts and payoff path, net worth, months of consistent behaviour, stress tests at higher rates, life events ahead?
- **E5** What is the single most valuable item on that list to a lender, in your view? To a broker? To the buyer?
- **E6** Is the value in the snapshot (today's numbers) or the track record (how the buyer behaved over months)?
- **E7** How many months of history would make it meaningful?
- **E8** At which point in the journey is it used: years before (nurture), at decision in principle, at full application, at underwriting, after completion (early warning)?
- **E9** Does it aim to change the lending decision, speed the decision up, reduce fall-through, or widen who can be considered? Which one first?
- **E10** Who is it for when the answer is "not yet"? What does the buyer get, and what does the business get?

### E2. Trust and verification

- **E11 [core]** Today the data is typed in by the user. Why would a lender rely on it? What would make them?
- **E12** What could be verified, and how: payslips, bank statements uploaded, open banking through a regulated partner, HMRC records, credit file, employer confirmation, lender's own account data?
- **E13** Which verification step are you prepared to build or buy first?
- **E14** How would you stop a buyer gaming it (entering the numbers that pass)?
- **E15** How would you show the difference between "user-entered", "user-evidenced" and "independently verified" in the output?
- **E16** If a figure in the pack is wrong and a lender relied on it, who is responsible? What do you want the contract to say?
- **E17** What would a lender's model risk or credit risk team ask before allowing any new input into a decision? Do you know anyone who could tell you?
- **E18** What evidence would prove it predicts anything (for example fewer failed applications, fewer arrears)? How long would that evidence take to gather, and with whom?

### E3. How it fits what lenders must already do

- **E19** What do you know about how lenders assess affordability today (income verification, committed and basic spending, stress rates, modelled spending figures)? Where did you learn it?
- **E20** Where in that process is the gap you fill: the facts, the speed, the confidence, or the customers who never reach the process?
- **E21** Would a lender use it in place of something, on top of something, or only before their process starts?
- **E22** Lenders already buy open banking affordability tools and credit bureau data. Why would they add Vault-It rather than ask their existing supplier?
- **E23** Is the better customer the lender (changes decisions slowly) or the broker (uses it tomorrow)?
- **E24** Could the pack be designed to drop into the forms brokers and lenders already use? Which forms or systems have you seen?

### E4. The buyer's side of it

- **E25 [core]** Why would a buyer want their Vault-It record shared with a lender or broker? What do they gain?
- **E26** Would a buyer behave differently in the app if they knew a lender might see it? Does that help or harm the product?
- **E27** Who decides what is shared, when, with whom, and for how long? Can they take it back?
- **E28** Could sharing ever count against a buyer? What is your rule when it would?
- **E29** Is it the buyer's passport (they carry it to any lender) or a lender's tool (one lender sees it)? Which is the better business?
- **E30** Would buyers pay for a verified pack themselves? Have you asked any?
