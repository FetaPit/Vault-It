# v07-house-engine (Stream A: E12 to E15)
Owner's words: "The main issue is the 3D engine, the animation for the house builder is not working. It's too slow. ... only the last course of bricks, the last row, is animated upon loading, and the rest act as one 3D model already loaded. But I like the animations, it's just really slow. ... it looks quite low fidelity, the wireframe, so the wireframe should be quite sharp. And the background, the ground plane animation isn't working, so there should be a subtle rectangle spreading out around the house, like on the net worth 3D viewport."
The idea: the house is already there the moment you look, sharp as a drawing, and only your newest course is laid in front of you (Pedro A, 1 Nov).
Who and when: the tester tonight on the preview, demo data, phone first, first 30 s on the House tab.
Chosen: Pedro A1 (full pixel ratio at rest first, LineMaterial only if the eaves shot fails), A2 (gold ping in lite, 0.45 to 0, reduced motion static), A6 with the addendum cuts (no sweep band; "Watch it built" left for v0.7.1 since it needs new markup under the viewport, Stream B's area).
Build notes: src/hb3d/viewer.js (_dprFor: min(devicePixelRatio, 2) at rest, 1.25 on a phone while bricks land, flying or a finger is down, back 300 ms after; openAt(p0, p1); shader warm-up frames into the hidden canvas), src/hb3d/brand.js (brandOpenFrom: bricks since last visit capped at the course being laid, first visit lays the top course; opening stagger min(40 ms, 1.2 s / bricks in course); gold ring in lite at most 4 frames a second; settled() means on screen and at rest), index.html hooks (still picture over the GL canvas until the first frame, 200 ms fade; 3D starts only once the stage is in view; per-device vi_hb_seen note; demo house state re-read after the demo seeds it).
