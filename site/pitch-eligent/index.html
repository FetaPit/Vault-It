THE SOLUTION · WHO IT HELPS MOST

Built for brains that find distant goals hard to see.

15–20%

of people are estimated to be neurodivergent. Time-blindness and executive-function load make a ten-year goal feel unreal.

3 to 4% of UK adults have ADHD, about 1.9 million (NICE). 31% of people with ADHD struggle with debt, against 11% of those without (Monzo and YouGov, 2022).

What Vault-It does

Turns the goal into a place: one date, a house you can orbit, and a visible response to every action. The next step is one brick, not a ten-year plan.

The evidence

  • AR improved money problem solving for autistic students (Root et al., 2022)
  • 33-study meta-analysis: large gains from AR and VR in autism (Karami et al., 2021)
  • Review: AR improved executive function and attention (Children, 2025)
  • More likely to be in problem debt, less confident with money (StepChange, 2025)

Strong mechanism evidence, not yet tested in a money app. That is our study to run: a University of Lincoln pilot with 250 to 750 participants, through an Innovate UK bid.

THE RESULT · FOR THE PARTNER

Institutions pay to reach tomorrow’s mortgage customers early.

Years earlier

In Sam’s example the deposit stage lasts 45 months. A building society meets Sam in month one of it, not at the mortgage application.

One pilot, one measure

90 days at £2,500 to £5,000. The measure: time from Stage 0 to Stage 3, from consented, anonymised journeys.

Low risk to try

No bank data and no integration: users type their own numbers. Consent first, revocable any time. That keeps a pilot low-risk for a lender’s supplier checks.

What white label costs us

Running cost is about 2p per user a month. The real cost is trust: Cyber Essentials Plus, a pen test, ISO 27001 when a partner asks. Set-up fees and the security budget pay for it.
