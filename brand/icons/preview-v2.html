Iconography v2

Same family, sharper eyes.

112 icons redrawn on the same 24 grid with the same ids, so they drop into every place v1 sits. Each one now has a lit core: the one part that names it, filled brighter so you can tell icons apart at a glance. Chamfered corners, signal nodes and fine tick lines carry the tech feel. One colour per icon, set by its domain.

Anatomy

Grid24 units, 2 unit safe margin. Same ids as v1 (vi-home, vi-vault and so on).
Hairline1.1 stroke, round caps. Steps up to 1.5 under 24px.
Body14% fill of the icon's colour under the outline.
Lit core42% fill on the part that names the icon: the lock body, the dial, the built courses. Built means lit.
Detail0.6 lines at 55%: ticks, seams, rivets, HUD brackets. Dropped under 24px.
Signal nodeSmall solid dot on the active point. Survives at 16px.
CornersRectangles are chamfered, not rounded. That cut corner is the pack's futurist signature.
ColourOne hue per icon, from the colour code. Commitment reads in the lock: lit and shut means committed, open and unlit means variable.
