Where it goes

  • Use for display headings, the net worth hero, stat-strip figures, tab labels at 12 px and up, deck and comms headlines.
  • Keep DM Sans for body copy, the FCA disclaimer line, form inputs and dense tables of text.
  • Hatch cuts only at 48 px and up: the hero number, one headline per screen, the tagline on decks. Never in tables, buttons or labels.
  • The wordmark and brick hyphen stay as artwork. Do not reset the logo in this font.
  • Use the minus sign (−) for debt so "−£300" reads as money owed.
