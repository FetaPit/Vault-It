Hero line, Night

Get out of the red and into your home.

A squared techno sans built from straight edges and 45 degree chamfers, with hatched extrusion faces running down and to the right. The big chamfers sit top-left and bottom-right, so every cut runs forward like a "/". This line is set in Heading and switches to the Hero cut once it renders at 96 px or more.

Where it goes

  • Heading (D+) for every heading, whatever its size (owner, 12 Nov 2026): page and section titles, card titles, deck and comms headlines, and the net worth hero figure. It stays legible down to about 18 px.
  • Hero (D++) for the hero line only, 96 px and up, one per page or slide. When the line renders under 96 px, switch it to Heading with a media query or clamp().
  • From 40 px add .vf-heading-lg (letter-spacing -0.01em, word-spacing -0.03em). Go no tighter.
  • DM Sans for everything else: body copy, labels, buttons, form inputs, tables, small figures and the FCA disclaimer line. Share Tech Mono stays for small labels where the site already uses it.
  • The wordmark is artwork drawn from Heading. Use the files in brand/logo/ and do not retype it.
  • Use the minus sign (−) for debt so "−£300" reads as money owed.
