Check-in

+150 XP · every 4 weeks

Your model is rebuilt from what you log here. Tape measurements set the size of each part, the four photos set the shape, Hume sets fat versus lean.

Tape (cm, relaxed)

Scale and height

Photos stay in this browser and are never uploaded. In this prototype the model is fitted to your tape and Hume numbers; fitting the shape to the photos comes with the app build.

For an accurate check-in

  • Phone upright at hip height, 2.5 m away. Lean it on a shelf and use the 10 second timer.
  • Fitted clothes or underwear, hair up, shoes off. A-pose: arms about 20° out.
  • Tape snug, not tight. Waist at the navel, arm at mid-bicep relaxed, thigh just below the glute.
  • Same time every check-in: morning, before food. Consistency matters more than anything else.

How points work

You earn 1 skill point per level, and the weekly boss drops one more. Each skill needs the one before it on its branch. Skills change how the game plays: new quest types, XP bonuses, streak freezes.

Octalysis coverage

Top drives (white hat) make you feel good about playing. Bottom drives (black hat) create urgency. For health we lean white hat and use black hat lightly, so the game never punishes rest or under-eating.

Guardrails

  • No reward for big deficits. Fuel quests pay for hitting a range, never for eating less.
  • Rest is a quest. Rest days and good sleep earn XP and streak freezes, so skipping training when wrecked never breaks the streak.
  • Recovery sets difficulty. Low HP (poor sleep, high resting HR) swaps today's quests for easier ones.
  • Losses are capped. The boss heals 5% per missed day. Levels and skills are never taken away.
