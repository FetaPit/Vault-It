## Voice (from the brand book, "Brand story and voice")
- Plain English. If a mate would not say it, we do not either.
- UK spelling, pounds first, real numbers over adjectives.
- Short sentences. Active verbs. Sentence case, except labels.
- Progress is physical: bricks, rows, walls, keys.
- Always show: "Not regulated by the FCA. Not financial advice."

Say this, not that:
- Lay the bricks. Not: Submit your contribution.
- You have £8.5k of £24.0k. Keep going. Not: Your deposit trajectory is suboptimal.
- Keys are yours. Not: Property acquisition goal achieved.
