03 · About

Overview

Overview
Vault·It shows you the way from debt to your own front door.

Put your numbers in. Your debts, your savings, your deposit. Vault·It turns every pound you save into bricks on a 3D house and gives you a keys date. No bank login. You type the numbers in and you stay in control.

Why it exists
Most people in debt have no date for owning a home. The average first-time buyer is now 34. New StepChange clients owe £16,874 on average. A goal ten years away feels unreal, especially if your brain finds distant goals hard to hold onto. So we made it something you can see, and build, one brick at a time.

Debt, then deposit, then keys.

Who it's for
People paying off debt who want to buy a home. And the building societies, brokers and debt charities who help them, through shared dashboards and white label.

Where we are
Live in alpha. Self-funded and built in Lincoln by Pete Thickett, designer and founder of PT Live Design. Accepted into Web Summit's startup programme and supported by the Business Lincolnshire Growth Hub.

Day 11 · Built for brains like ours
If a goal is ten years away, for a lot of us it may as well not exist.

Time-blindness is real. If you're neurodivergent, holding onto something that far off and acting on it every month is hard work.

So we stopped asking people to imagine it.

In Vault·It your home is right there in front of you, getting built every time you save. You can spin it round. You can see the gap.

We're working on research with the University of Lincoln to test it properly. Until then, try it and tell us if it helps.
