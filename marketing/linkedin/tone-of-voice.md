# Pete's LinkedIn tone of voice

Use this for every LinkedIn post written for Pete or Vault-It. Pete supplied the reference post below as his "perfect tone of voice for LinkedIn" (5 Nov 2026).

## What it sounds like
- Warm, upbeat and professional. Grateful and excited, not casual "mate" talk.
- Opens with a hook and the news straight away ("Newsflash!"), then logistics early (date, time, venue).
- Thanks people by full name and title ("Massive thanks to Dr Slava Baranovskiy for the opportunity").
- Business vocabulary is welcome: "moving the needle", "envisage", "holistic", "utilised with effect", "potential B2B applications".
- Longer, flowing sentences are fine. First person, personal story, then the product.
- Names what the product does in full: debt management, budgeting, digital wealth inventory, 3D house that builds as you save.
- Clear calls to action: sign up at vault-it.co, connect beforehand, B2B chats with first-time buyer, debt charity and building society contacts.
- Sign-off: "Pete" then "Founder @ Vault-It.co".
- Emojis used sparingly as markers (📍 🎟️).

## Do not
- Put words in someone's mouth or invent quotes. Check any attribution.
- Invent facts, dates or offers. Check trial and pricing claims against vault-it.co.
